# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides quick links to the OctoAcme project management process documents in this repository and a short summary of how the team approaches delivery.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery model with clear ownership and measurable outcomes. Projects begin with initiation, where the team confirms the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and creates a simple one-pager with goals, timeline, risks, and resource needs. Once approved, the team moves into planning, where work is broken into a prioritized backlog, estimated, and mapped to a Definition of Done with release milestones and dependencies.

During execution, the team uses a project board to manage workflow, runs daily standups and weekly syncs, and follows a pull request process that emphasizes small, reviewable changes, CI validation, and explicit acceptance criteria. The project manager and product lead coordinate timelines, risk tracking, and communication, while developers, QA, and stakeholders collaborate on delivery quality and approval. The team also documents risks and escalations, maintaining a clear path for issues that require broader coordination or sponsor review.

Release and deployment follow a controlled checklist with pre-release requirements, smoke tests, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification to reduce operational risk. After each sprint, milestone, or important incident, the team conducts a retrospective to capture lessons learned, define action items, and feed improvements back into the backlog and project plan. Across all stages, OctoAcme emphasizes communication, accountability, testing, and continuous improvement so work stays aligned with customer value and delivery goals.
